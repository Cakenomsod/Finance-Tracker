# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are Thai personal-finance users who manage money across solo spending, shared obligations, and group activities. Contexts include: logging daily expenses on the go, settling debts with friends or partners without awkward follow-ups, and coordinating trip costs. Power users lean on AI parsing, LINE bot capture, and analytics for speed and clarity. The app serves individuals and small social groups—not businesses or accountants.

## Product Purpose

Finance Tracker is a personal finance operating system: track where money goes, split shared costs fairly, and capture transactions with minimal friction. Success means users always know their balance and spending patterns, can settle up with friends in seconds, and log expenses faster than they forget them (via quick add, AI, receipts, or LINE).

## Brand Personality

Friendly and social on the surface—approachable, cooperative, group-first—backed by sharp, efficient tooling underneath. It should feel like a trusted friend who keeps the numbers straight: warm enough for shared debts and trips, fast enough for daily capture, never cute or gamified.

## Anti-references

- Overly playful fintech: neon gradients, mascots, streaks, badges-as-gamification, confetti on every action
- Generic SaaS dashboards: navy-and-gold hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, gradient text, side-stripe callouts
- Stuffy corporate banking apps: dense jargon, cold institutional tone, slow multi-step flows for simple tasks

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity before ceremony** — Numbers, balances, and who-owes-whom should be immediately readable. No decorative chrome that competes with the data.
2. **Social without friction** — Debts, trips, and friend requests should feel cooperative, not confrontational. Language and layout reduce awkwardness around money between people.
3. **Speed to capture** — Logging an expense must be faster than forgetting it. Quick add, AI, LINE, and receipt flows are first-class, not afterthoughts.
4. **Bilingual by default** — Thai workflows (฿, Paotang, local copy) and English UI coexist naturally; neither language feels bolted on.
5. **Restrained personality** — Friendly and efficient, never playful-fintech. Delight comes from how well the tool works, not from mascots or gamification.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA as baseline for contrast, focus, and interactive targets
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support for animations and transitions
- Thai + English bilingual readability: legible mixed-script typography, adequate line length, and copy that works in both contexts
